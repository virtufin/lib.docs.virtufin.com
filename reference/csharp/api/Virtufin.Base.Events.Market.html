Namespace Virtufin.Base.Events.Market

Classes

MarketEvent

Sealed ADT of raw market observations — what the feed emits. Two cases: MarketEvent.TickReceived (best bid/ask update) and MarketEvent.OrderBookReceived (depth snapshot/diff). Topics use the naming scheme act.market.{entity}.{event} or hyp.{selector}.market.{entity}.{event}.

MarketEvent.OrderBookReceived

Depth snapshot or diff from the order book feed.

MarketEvent.TickReceived

Best bid/ask update from the feed.

RichMarketEvent

Enriched market events — derived signals (candles, VWAP, volatility) plus pass-through for raw MarketEvents. Both flow through the same act.market. / hyp.{selector}.market. topics since they share the IMarketEvent marker.

RichMarketEvent.Base

Pass-through for a raw MarketEvent.

RichMarketEvent.CandleClosed

OHLCV candle closed (derived from a tick window).

RichMarketEvent.VWAPComputed

Volume-weighted average price (derived from tick stream).

RichMarketEvent.VolatilityEstimate

Realised volatility estimate (e.g. standard deviation of log returns).
